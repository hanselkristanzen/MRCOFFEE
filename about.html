Our Story

Susanto Liman

CEO & Founder of MR.COFFEE

Susanto Liman, a lifelong coffee enthusiast, embarked on a journey to transform his passion into a haven for coffee lovers. With a vision to create more than just a coffee shop, Susanto founded MR.COFFEE as a place where quality, artistry, and community converge. His philosophy is simple: every bean has a story, every cup is a masterpiece, and every customer is part of the MR.COFFEE family.

"From the moment I first experienced the magic of a perfectly brewed cup, I knew I wanted to share that joy with others. MR.COFFEE is the culmination of that dream – a place where the aroma of fresh coffee blends with laughter and conversation. We meticulously source our beans from the finest growers, ensuring that each sip reflects our commitment to excellence. Our baristas are not just coffee makers; they are artists who pour their hearts into every creation. We aim to be a sanctuary for dreamers, a hub for creatives, and a warm, welcoming spot for anyone seeking a moment of inspiration or connection. This is more than a business; it's our passion, and we're thrilled to share it with you, both in our cozy shop and now, in the digital world."

Our Mission

"To make all taste coffee every day, by providing an exceptional and inspiring coffee experience that enriches the lives of our customers and community."

Our Vision

"To be the most beloved coffee community, inspiring connections and creativity one cup at a time, both locally and globally."

The MR.COFFEE Journey

In the heart of a bustling city, MR.COFFEE was born from a simple idea: to create a coffee shop that felt like a second home. It wasn't just about serving coffee; it was about crafting experiences. Our journey began with a quest for the world's finest coffee beans, leading us to remote mountaintops where unique flavors awaited.

Our first shop, with its mismatched chairs, walls adorned with customer doodles, and the ever-present aroma of freshly brewed wonders, quickly became a local favorite. It was a place where baristas were artists, each cup a personalized creation. We fostered an atmosphere of warmth and quirkiness, where strangers became friends over shared tables and steaming mugs.

MR.COFFEE became synonymous with more than just exceptional coffee. It stood for connection, creativity, and a pause from the everyday hustle. It was a cozy haven for dreamers, a quiet corner for writers, and a vibrant spot for conversations that sparked new ideas. The laughter, the stories, and the genuine smiles exchanged within our walls became the true essence of our brand.

As our community grew, so did our desire to share the MR.COFFEE magic further. This website is our next chapter, an invitation to experience the warmth, stories, and creativity of MR.COFFEE, no matter where you are. We're excited to bring our passion for coffee and connection to the digital world, building a broader community and inspiring moments of joy far beyond our city limits.
